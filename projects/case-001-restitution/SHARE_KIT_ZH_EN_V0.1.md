**《中国欠债 通告全球》：雪峰发表追偿通告**

2026年11月3日，生命禅院导游雪峰发表《〈中国欠债 通告全球〉（追讨债务书）》，面向中国政府、联合国相关机构、各国政府、使领馆和国际组织，提出公开追偿要求。

通告回顾了生命禅院自2013年前后以来在中国境内多个家园的经历，列出了中国境内16处家园，并讲述泰国家园成员离境的情况。两者的处置经过并不完全相同。通告还表示，2025年7月起约80位成员遭跨省抓捕，2026年10月23日林州市人民法院作出涉及六位成员的刑事判决，并有罚金与财物处置。

雪峰对定罪依据、被认定为违法所得的财产如何处理等提出质疑，同时按家园建设损失、没收款项、成员损失和关押损失列出追偿金额，扣除此前所称两笔补偿后，要求人民币67,177,185元。

通告最后邀请相关国际机构、记者及人权组织独立核查，表示可以依法提供账目、合同、判决书及成员证言供查阅。

**Xuefeng issues public notice seeking compensation**

On 3 November 2026, Xuefeng, the guide of Lifechanyuan, issued a public notice titled “China Owes a Debt: A Notice to the World.” Addressed to the Chinese government, United Nations bodies, foreign governments and international organizations, the statement sets out a compensation demand and calls for independent examination.

Xuefeng recounts actions affecting 16 communal homes in China since approximately 2013. A separate seventeenth site, in Thailand, is included in the broader account because its residents reportedly departed under pressure. These are distinct events with different circumstances. The notice also reports that approximately 80 members were apprehended in operations beginning in July 2025. It refers to a criminal judgment delivered by the Linzhou People's Court on 23 October 2026 involving six members, along with fines and orders concerning assets.

The statement questions the legal and factual basis of the criminal proceedings and the classification and disposition of certain assets. It presents four categories of alleged losses and seeks a total of RMB 67,177,185 after deducting two previously reported payments.

Finally, Xuefeng invites international experts, journalists and human rights organizations to review relevant records independently. He states that accounts, agreements, court documents and witness statements may be made available within an appropriate legal framework.
