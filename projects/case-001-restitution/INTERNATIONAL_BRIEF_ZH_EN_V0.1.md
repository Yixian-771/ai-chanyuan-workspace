**关于生命禅院成员被抓捕、羁押、定罪及共同生活家园受损情况的事实摘要（待核验稿）**

### 1. 情况简述

## English | Preliminary brief for independent review

### Subject
**Preliminary factual brief concerning reported arrests, detention, criminal convictions, and disruption of communal homes associated with Lifechanyuan**

### 1. Background
On 3 November 2026, Xuefeng, the guide of Lifechanyuan, issued a public statement titled *“China Owes a Debt: A Notice to the World.”* According to the statement, communal homes associated with Lifechanyuan in China have faced closures, property losses, or other state actions since approximately 2013. The statement lists **16 sites in China**. A subsequent clarification from a member counts **one additional home in Thailand** as the seventeenth site, citing the departure of its residents under pressure. These different events require separate verification and should not be presented as identical forms of state action.

### 2. Alleged arrests and court proceedings
The statement reports that approximately **80 members** were apprehended by police authorities associated with Linzhou, Henan Province, beginning in **July 2025**, and that some members remained detained as of 3 November 2026. It further reports that on **23 October 2026**, the Linzhou People's Court delivered a criminal judgment, case reference **(2026) Yu 0581 Xing Chu 172**, concerning six members, with custodial sentences, fines, and orders concerning seized or frozen assets.

Xuefeng has subsequently stated that the summary was drawn from a **53-page judgment**. The present brief relies on his attributed public account; full judicial records and person-specific information would be supplied through authorized channels as appropriate. The current custody and appeal status of each person has not yet been independently established for public release.

### 3. Questions warranting examination
- What specific acts, evidence, and legal provisions supported each arrest, detention, conviction, and financial order?
- Were the persons' freedoms of thought, belief, expression, and association appropriately respected?
- Did the detention and trial procedures meet applicable domestic and international standards?
- What remedies are available in relation to property seizure, freezing, and confiscation?

These are **questions for independent examination**, not findings already established by an independent body.

### 4. Assistance sought
We propose seeking independent legal review and, where appropriate, consideration by competent international human rights mechanisms of any **person-specific allegations of arbitrary detention or related rights violations**, together with recommendations for effective remedies.

### 5. Evidence, consent and safety
The author reports access to the full judgment and supporting records. Identifiable individual case files, custody dates, authorizations, and domestic legal remedies should only be provided to an appropriate recipient **after informed consent and a confidentiality/safety review**. They are not part of this public working draft.

### 6. Separate monetary claims
The statement makes a monetary claim totaling **RMB 67,177,185**. The arithmetic can be checked, but legal entitlement has **not** been determined. Monetary remedies require their own legal analysis and appropriate proceedings. No unauthorized asset transfers are contemplated.
