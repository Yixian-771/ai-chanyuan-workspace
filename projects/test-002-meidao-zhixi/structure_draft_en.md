## 0. Positioning (editor's note)

- **Audience**: members of the public meeting Second Home for the first time.
- **One line**: show first how a group of people make ordinary days beautiful, then explain the production-and-living procedures behind that beauty.
- **Tone**: calm, concrete, honest. No hype. Hardships stay in (long winters, too few hands).
- **Placement**: a feature page inside civ3.lifecosmos.org, using the existing navigation. No new site.

## 1. Hero

- **Headline**: Breathtakingly Beautiful
- **Subhead**: What kind of life is Second Home creating?
- **Intro (editor's note, ~50 words)**: On a lake in northern Canada and on Jeju Island in Korea, a small group of people live without wages or private savings. They grow vegetables, repair machines, host guests and celebrate together. What follows are their own records of ordinary days. Judge the beauty for yourself.
